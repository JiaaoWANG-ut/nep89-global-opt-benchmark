NEP89 extension: 100 seeds

中文

4 additional systems, 22 algorithms, 100 seeds per cell. This four-system ranking is separate from the published 45-system study; its scores are not directly comparable because the benchmark sets differ.

Score: 100 times the mean of clip(1 + log10(ERT_best / ERT) / 3, 0, 1). ERT counts steps to the first energy at or below E_ref + tolerance; timeouts without a hit are charged the full budget. Intervals use a normal approximation from the standard deviation of 1000 within-cell bootstrap samples.

E_ref is the lowest stored energy from any campaign for the current benchmark hash whose guard and wall energy is at most 0.01 eV. Guard-active runs are scored by energy, as in the original study; the counts below should be examined for artefacts. The reference is a putative NEP89 minimum, not a DFT global minimum.

GOFEE and SurrBH had a 3,600-second limit per seed. Time limits may affect hit rates and rankings, especially for costly systems.

System selection and limitations

The four systems are Al13, Pt8 on CeO2(111), Au8 in BEA, and variable-cell Al4O6. They were selected from earlier candidates, not randomly sampled; Pt8/CeO2 and Al4O6 had previously been set aside as relatively easy. This small set is not representative of all structure-search problems. Scores are also not directly comparable with the first 100-seed extension.

The CeO2 slab and BEA framework are frozen. NEP89 does not model ceria reduction or oxygen vacancies, and metal/zeolite binding is qualitative. The surface and zeolite reference structures extend outside their initial sampling regions; relaxation is not constrained to those regions. Passing the listed checks does not establish physical correctness or global optimality.
