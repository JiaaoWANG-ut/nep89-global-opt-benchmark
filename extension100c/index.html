NEP89 extension: 100 seeds

中文

4 additional systems, 26 algorithms, 100 seeds per cell. This four-system ranking is separate from the published 45-system study; its scores are not directly comparable because the benchmark sets differ.

Score: 100 times the mean of clip(1 + log10(ERT_best / ERT) / 3, 0, 1). ERT counts steps to the first energy at or below E_ref + tolerance; timeouts without a hit are charged the full budget. Intervals use a normal approximation from the standard deviation of 1000 within-cell bootstrap samples.

E_ref is the lowest stored energy from any campaign for the current benchmark hash whose guard and wall energy is at most 0.01 eV. Guard-active runs are scored by energy, as in the original study; the counts below should be examined for artefacts. The reference is a putative NEP89 minimum, not a DFT global minimum.

GOFEE and SurrBH had a 3,600-second limit per seed. Time limits may affect hit rates and rankings, especially for costly systems.

System selection and experimental methods

The four systems are Cu13, variable-cell Al8, Pd7 on MgO(001), and Au4 initialized in a CHA zeolite. These manually selected systems are not a random or representative sample. The surface and zeolite hosts were also used during algorithm development, so they are not unseen hosts. In a separate five-seed pilot with BH and SurrBH, all 40 runs hit their pilot reference window; the systems may be relatively easy.

The 26 compared methods comprise 22 previously frozen algorithms and two experimental families, each tested both before and after adding a hosted-site move. They are not four independent inventions. A ten-seed surface-development ablation gave 3/10 versus 4/10 hits for the diversity family, and 3/10 versus 5/10 for path relinking; these small development samples do not establish an improvement. The methods combine established ideas; literature novelty and general superiority have not been established.

The hosts are frozen; the CHA source CIF provenance is unknown. Initial placement regions are not confinement constraints, and two of ten zeolite pilot centers lay outside the initial region; that alone proves neither escape nor confinement. NEP89 is not DFT, and guard-free structures may still be unphysical. Scores from different benchmark sets are not directly comparable.

Hosted-move ablations · Frozen protocol · System pilot audit · Final reference force checks · Frozen input hashes.

The additional saved-candidate geometry audit checked 10,086 best structures in the reference window from the formal campaign and pilot; none failed its energy, guard, distance, connectivity or applicable volume checks. All 2,387 zeolite candidates had at least one atom outside the initial region, and 116 had their cluster center outside the initial sphere. These region observations alone do not establish cage escape or confinement. Intermediate trajectory geometries were not saved and cannot be assessed by this audit.
